# Start Here

Welcome to Studiograph. This is your team's shared workspace — documents, designs, decks, and data all live here, and a built-in AI helps you make them. You work by asking: describe what you want in plain language, point at a template, and refine the result.

## What you can make

| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Build a slide deck | [[presentation-templates-overview]] |
| Write a designed document — a report, a letterhead doc, a newsletter | [[publication-templates-overview]] |
| Launch a landing page | [[landing-page-templates-overview]] |
| Turn facts and numbers into a graphic | [[infographic-templates-overview]] |
| Explain how something works — flowcharts, org charts, system maps | [[diagram-templates-overview]] |
| Chart data and build a dashboard | [[working-with-data-overview]] |
| Post to LinkedIn, Instagram, X or YouTube | [[social-media-overview]] |
| Sharpen everything — writing, charts, colours, prompts | [[core-skills-overview]] |
| Make all of it look like *your* company | [[your-brand-overview]] |

Every folder here works the same way: an **overview** that shows you how to ask, a **skills** folder holding the templates, and an **examples** folder with a finished piece for every template — open the examples first.

## Three words worth knowing

- **Entry** — anything saved in the workspace: a note, a person, a project, a report. Entries come in three formats: **docs** (written pages), **sheets** (tables of data), and **canvases**.
- **Canvas** — the designed format: a deck, a paginated document, a graphic, a working page. Everything the templates in this folder make is a canvas.
- **Skill** — a document that tells the AI how something should look or be done. Every template in this folder is a skill. You use one by naming it in a request.

## Your first fifteen minutes

1. **Make a deck.** Open [[presentation-templates-overview]], pick a theme from the examples, and ask for a six-slide deck on something real — this week's project update works.
2. **Make a document.** Open [[publication-templates-overview]] and ask for a one-page memo using `minimal-report` — then put your logo in the header.
3. **Teach it your brand.** Open [[your-brand-overview]] and fill in the brand skill with your colours, fonts, and voice. Everything you make from now on can use it.

This folder is shared with everyone in your workspace. Duplicate anything into your own folder before customizing it heavily — the originals stay as a clean reference for the whole team.

## Where this folder comes from

Everything here is maintained openly at [github.com/studiographai/getting-started-library](https://github.com/studiographai/getting-started-library) — the canonical, versioned source for these templates and skills.

You don't need it to use anything above. But if you want to see what changed and when, or hear about new templates as they land, watch that repository for releases.
