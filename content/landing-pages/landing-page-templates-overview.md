# Landing Page Templates — Overview

Each landing page template in this folder is a **skill**. A skill is a document that tells the AI how something should look — here, that means the sections, colours and type of a marketing page. In this folder, "skill" and "landing page template" mean the same thing.

## 1. Pick a template

The example pages are in `landing-pages` → `examples` — a live, responsive page for every template. Open one and drag the window narrower; it reflows like the real thing.

| Template | Style | Best for | Example |
|---|---|---|---|

| [[freelancer-landing]] | Personal portfolio — avatar, skills, project tiles | Freelancers, personal brand sites | [[freelancer-landing-app]] |
| [[grayscale-landing]] | Monochrome + gold editorial — full-bleed masthead | Design studios, creative portfolios | [[grayscale-landing-app]] |
| [[creative-landing]] | Colour-blocked, playful — skewed shapes | Creative studios, campaigns | [[creative-landing-app]] |
| [[new-age-landing]] | Dark app-marketing — split hero, device mockup | Mobile/SaaS launches, product pages | [[new-age-landing-app]] |
| [[coming-soon-landing]] | Minimal single screen — countdown, email capture | Pre-launch pages, waitlists | [[coming-soon-landing-app]] |

## 2. Ask for your page

```text
Build a landing page using the `new-age-landing` skill.

Product: Fieldnote, a mobile app for trail runners
Sections: hero with app mockup, three feature highlights, an email signup
Tone: energetic and direct

Show me the section outline before you build it.
```

Name the skill exactly as it is written on its entry — `agency-landing`, `coming-soon-landing`. That is what loads the design.

Have your logo, product shots, and copy points ready — attach or paste them into the request. If you have a brand skill ([[your-brand-overview]]), name it too and the page comes out in your colours and type.

## 3. Adjust

Ask by section: *"swap the hero image"*, *"make the features three columns"*, *"darken the footer."*

The page works at every size — check it on a narrow window before you share it.
