# Northwind Studio Brand (Example)

**This is a worked example, not your brand.** Northwind Studio is invented. It exists to show what [[brand-skill-template]] looks like filled in properly — specific values, stated rules, real before/after pairs. Read it, then write your own.

---

## Identity

**Organisation:** Northwind Studio
**What we do, in one line:** We design wayfinding and identity systems for cultural institutions.
**Audience:** Museum directors, civic clients, and the architects we partner with.

## Colours

| Role | Hex | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| ink | #14181F | body text, headlines |
| paper | #FBFAF7 | page and slide backgrounds |
| muted | #6E7580 | captions, secondary text, axis labels |
| accent | #C2410C | the one thing that should draw the eye |
| line | #E2DED6 | rules, borders, gridlines |

**Rules:**
- The accent marks one or two elements per piece — a single figure, one call to action. On five things it marks nothing.
- Never set the accent on the ink background; it fails contrast. Use paper on ink instead.
- Photography sits on paper, never on accent.

## Typography

| Role | Family | Weight / size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| headline | Söhne Breit | 600, 40–56px | tight tracking (−0.02em), sentence case |
| body | Söhne | 400, 16–18px | line height 1.55 |
| caption / label | Söhne Mono | 500, 12–13px, uppercase 0.1em | eyebrows, figure labels |
| numerals | Söhne, tabular | 400 | charts and tables only |

**Fallbacks:** Georgia, serif / system-ui, sans-serif.

Söhne is licensed for web use and uploaded to this workspace as `.woff2`. Do not substitute Helvetica or Inter — the wide headline cut is doing real work in our identity.

## Logo

**Files:** `northwind-primary.svg` (ink on light), `northwind-reversed.svg` (paper on dark), `northwind-mark.svg` (compass mark alone)
**Clear space:** the height of the compass mark on all sides
**Minimum size:** 20px tall on screen, 10mm in print; below that use the mark alone
**Placement:** top left on documents, bottom left on slides, never centred
**Never:** recolour, outline, rotate, or place over photography without the reversed version

## Letterhead and furniture

**Header:** primary logo top left, first page only
**Footer:** Northwind Studio · 44 Harbour Road, Portland · northwindstudio.com
**Page numbers:** bottom right, "Page N of M", not on the cover
**Margins:** top 96 / sides 72 / bottom 84

## Voice

**We sound:** plain, specific, quietly confident. We describe what we did and what happened.
**We never sound:** breathless, mystical about design, or padded with process jargon.
**Person and tense:** first person plural, present tense. "We design" — not "Northwind Studio designs".

**Words we use:** wayfinding, legible, civic, craft, evidence
**Words we avoid:** journey, elevate, bespoke, iconic, delight, seamless

**Punctuation and format:** no exclamation marks. Sentence case headlines. Oxford comma. Numerals for 10 and above. Spell out percentages in prose, use % in charts.

**Before / after:**

> ✗ We elevated the visitor journey through a bespoke wayfinding solution.
> ✓ We redrew the museum's signage. Visitors found the galleries 40% faster.

> ✗ Our iconic identity system delights audiences at every touchpoint.
> ✓ The identity works at 10mm on a badge and at six metres on a banner.

> ✗ Leveraging deep cultural insight, we crafted a seamless brand experience.
> ✓ We spent three weeks watching how people actually move through the building.

## Applying this

- Colours and type replace a template's neutral defaults.
- Layout stays with the template unless it directly conflicts.
- Voice governs every word written into the piece.
- Logo and letterhead apply to documents and decks automatically.

## What makes this one work

Three things worth copying into your own:

1. **Values, not adjectives.** "#C2410C" and "Söhne 600, 40–56px" are executable. "Warm and modern" is not.
2. **Stated prohibitions.** The banned word list and "never on the ink background" prevent more bad output than any amount of positive description.
3. **Before/after pairs.** Three examples teach voice better than a paragraph about it.
