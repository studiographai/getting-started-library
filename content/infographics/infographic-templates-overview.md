# Infographic Templates — Overview

Each infographic template in this folder is a **skill**. A skill is a document that tells the AI how something should look — here, that means the layout, colours and type of a graphic. In this folder, "skill" and "infographic template" mean the same thing.

## 2. Ask for your graphic

```text
Make an infographic using the `timeline-flow` skill.

Topic: our 2026 roadmap
Format: square, for social

Five milestones with one line of context each. Show me the wording before you build it.
```

Name the skill exactly as it is written on its entry — `timeline-flow`, `swot-quadrant`, `funnel-flow`. That is what loads the design.

Say what shape you need — square for a feed, portrait for a story, widescreen for a slide.

Name your brand skill alongside it ([[your-brand-overview]]) and the graphic comes out in your colours and type.

## 3. Adjust

Ask for changes by naming the part: *"make the third stat bigger"* or *"use our accent colour on the last row."*

These templates are deliberately restrained — one accent, plenty of space, no decoration that isn't carrying information. If a graphic feels crowded, the fix is usually fewer items rather than smaller type.

Explaining how something *works* rather than presenting facts? Use [[diagram-templates-overview]] instead.
